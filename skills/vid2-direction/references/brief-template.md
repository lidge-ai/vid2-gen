# Directing brief

| Decision | Fill with evidence |
|---|---|
| One takeaway | A single claim the viewer can repeat after 30 s |
| Audience/action | Who watches; what they should understand or do |
| Proof | Real feature, result, interaction or measurable fact shown on screen |
| Format | 1920×1080 or 1080×1920; fps; 15/30/45 s |
| Source inventory | Capture sessions, logo/font files, hero stills, licensed music |
| References | 1–2 links or local clips, each transcribed to shot grammar |
| Story arc | 0–5 s hook, 5–18 s build, 18–25 s proof, 25–30 s resolve (adapt to brief) |
| Readability | Minimum type size and safe margins for the destination |
| Audio | Track, BPM/offset, voice, key cut and SFX anchors |
| Review | Storyboard stills, proxy, QA, final QA |

Draft a scene table with columns: scene id, duration/frame range, on-screen evidence, dominant motion, headline, cue, transition. Keep scene IDs stable during iterations so cache reuse and feedback references remain useful.
