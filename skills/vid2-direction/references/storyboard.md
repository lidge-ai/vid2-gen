# Stills-first review

For each scene, make one still at its decisive frame. Include one still before and after each transition, plus the midpoint of a long camera move. Assemble a contact sheet in story order and read it without audio. The viewer should understand the claim, the real action, and the result from those frames alone.

Check: (1) real brand/UI assets are present; (2) headline is legible at half size; (3) the subject sits inside 5% safe margins; (4) no crop conceals a needed control; (5) motion direction carries across cuts; (6) visual energy rises and then has a deliberate hold. If one frame fails, revise that scene before rendering the whole video. After stills, review a proxy at normal speed with audio; a contact sheet cannot reveal rushed reading time or mistimed SFX.

When transcribing a reference, record timestamp, shot length in frames, scale of subject, camera start/end, transition type, text weight/size, and audible hit. Example: “2.0–4.5 s, medium product crop, x 0.48→0.55, 1.00→1.12× push, 0.3 s fade to next screen, impact at 4.5 s.” Replace the content with this product's real UI.
