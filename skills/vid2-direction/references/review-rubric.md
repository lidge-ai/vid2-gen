# Review rubric

Score each category 0–4 or `cannotDetermine`: `narrative` (claim and proof), `hierarchy` (where attention lands), `legibility` (text at delivery size), `continuity` (subject, palette, screen direction), `motion` (purpose and settling), `audioTiming` (picture/sound relation), and `technical` (render faults). For each finding, name a scene/time, severity, observation, evidence and a specific timeline edit. The scores are the vid2 review contract (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/020_analyze_and_review.md`), not a substitute for watching the film.

Trust evidence by source. Sampled `frames` can show hierarchy, visible text and particular seams, but cannot prove unsampled motion, actual UI behavior or sound. `dsp` owns loudness, low end and sync measurements. The optional `listener` informs timbre, groove, arrangement and mood; its sub-bass judgment is weak, so low-end listener remarks remain informational. Mark what cannot be determined rather than filling the gap with confidence. Review trust rules (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/020_analyze_and_review.md`).

Review a muted contact sheet for problem → action → proof → CTA, then motion strips around fast cuts, then the full film at normal listening level. Check first/middle/last frames and both sides of every important seam. Compare analysis cut and onset deltas with the intent table; verify a cited timestamp before changing the timeline. A numerical score never certifies that a claimed product action really occurred. Theory digest (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/003_theory_digest.md`), reference verification loop (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/001_reference_films.md`).
