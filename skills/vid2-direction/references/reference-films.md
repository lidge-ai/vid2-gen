# Measured reference films

The source notes and method (`devlog/_plan/260928_film_grammar/001_reference_films.md`) measured two public agent-made films with ffmpeg scene score > 0.3 and RMS-flux audio onsets. These are sample-specific observations, not genre targets; score thresholds, dissolves and effects affect detected shot boundaries.

| Film | Average shot length | Cuts within 50 ms of onset | Reusable construction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| donald, “Claude Pop” remake | 1.43 s | 58 % | constrained print palette; repeated protagonist; persistent counter; short strobe bursts |
| anabology, Opus 5.5 / moodboard film | 2.47 s | 62 % | section color arc; one orange accent; thin HUD and bottom ticker; beat-length shots |
| our opencodex mix before WP2 | 2.68 s | 7 % | shows why output onset audit matters |

The WP2 authored beat-cut audit later found 16/16 cuts on beat; that is grid alignment, not an onset percentage.
