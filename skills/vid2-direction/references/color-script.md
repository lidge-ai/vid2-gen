# Color script

Make one thumbnail per scene before choosing a look. Record `sceneId`, narrative function, dominant/background/accent colors, a neutral reference (skin, product UI white or gray), contrast strategy, look preset, and the intended transition to the next scene. A sample arc is restrained problem → accent enters → clean product proof → brand lockup. This is a house proposal, not a fixed color psychology rule. Pixar's colorscript practice and correction-versus-grading distinction inform it. Theory digest (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/003_theory_digest.md`).

Correct exposure and white balance across adjoining shots before applying a creative grade. Keep real UI proof readable and its status colors truthful. Check representative frames and both sides of seams for luma, saturation, clipping and text contrast; label a deliberate chapter color jump. Avoid a universal pass/fail Kelvin or saturation number without an output profile and reference set. Theory digest (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/003_theory_digest.md`).

`look:{"preset":"film"}` gives a film treatment; `riso` accepts a 2–6 color palette; `paper` warms the surface. A palette on film or paper is a validation issue. `strength:0` keeps the authored choice but skips the look chain. A consistent seed and palette help repeatability, while a recurring accent/HUD can carry identity through fast cuts. The measured references used constrained palettes: donald's print colors and anabology's orange accent across a changing section arc. Reference films (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/001_reference_films.md`).

For generated footage, record the actual prompt, model/version, approved reference, palette, seed and output dimensions. A seed alone is not a style lock; verify candidates in a contact sheet and keep product proof in real capture. Theory digest (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/003_theory_digest.md`).

| Example scene role | Background / accent | Neutral reference | Check at seam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem | restrained navy / muted amber | UI gray | avoid crushed detail |
| Action | deeper navy / active orange | product white | accent guides the eye |
| Proof | clean neutral / same orange | real UI white | preserve real status colors |
| CTA | brand field / orange | logo white | keep text contrast and hold |

This table is a proposed arc, not a measured property of either reference film. Theory digest (`devlog/_fin/260928_film_grammar/003_theory_digest.md`).
