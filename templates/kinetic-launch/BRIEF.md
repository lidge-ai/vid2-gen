# Kinetic launch brief

A 30-second launch film in the kinetic grammar: one idea per frame, words and icons as actors, rebuilt UI instead of screen
recordings, and sound that follows the motion. Fill in, in this order:

1. **The first action** a user takes (the `hook` field text) and **the pain** in one sentence whose last word survives the magic move.
2. **The promise** — one sentence, 3 short lines, read with the highlight sweep.
3. **Proof** — three things it does at once (chips) and **one honest number** you can defend (bars). Do not invent benchmarks.
4. **What it can do** — four to six verbs with icons (ticker) and **the result** (the hero image prompt or a real still).
5. **The end card** — name plus one command or URL.

Swap `sparkles` for your app icon: add an `image` source (for example `"app": {"type":"image","path":"media/icon.png"}`) and write
`{app}`; the `expand` token becomes `icon:app#0`. Keep scenes dark ↔ light every 6–12 s.
