# ima2-gen launch film — brief

**Claim:** ima2 is an image studio that runs on your machine and does many images at once.
**Proof shot:** the real ima2 web UI typing a prompt and producing the result (captured, not mocked).
**Look:** the kinetic grammar in the `vid2-direction` skill (`references/kinetic-grammar.md`): one idea per frame, words and icons as
actors, rebuilt UI for single moments, dark ↔ light rhythm, sound that follows motion.

| # | Scene | Built with | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook | `field` (grow, accent, cursor click, typing) | the first action: typing a prompt |
| 2 | problem | `kinetic` states (magic move) | tabs, logins, queues → "queues." |
| 3 | scatter | raw `stage` (14 "Generate +" pills) + motion blur, `kinetic` drop | one at a time |
| 4 | intro | `kinetic` + `expand` of the official app icon | the app opens |
| 5 | brand | ima2-generated light rays, camera push, `kinetic` blur, `fadewhite` | ima2 |
| 6 | studio | `kinetic` reading highlight on light | runs on your machine |
| 7 | providers | `kinetic` icons as words | GPT, Grok, Gemini |
| 8 | product | real capture in a `window` (typing at 1×, generation at 8×) + `chips` with connectors | the real app, jobs in parallel |
| 9 | bars | `bars` with count-up, entered through `iris` | 12 in flight vs one at a time |
| 10 | can | `ticker` with icons on light | what it can do |
| 11 | gallery | six ima2 images in `window` tiles, `zoomfrom` the cat-astronaut tile | made with ima2 |
| 12 | hero | the generated image full frame, prompt typed underneath | the result |
| 13 | local | `kinetic` scramble + `field` mask | local and private |
| 14 | end | icon + name, install command typed | npm i -g ima2-gen |
