# vid2 launch — brief

Message: **the edit is a file** — vid2 turns a declarative timeline plus real captures and generated assets into a finished, QA'd video.
Audience: developers who use coding agents (Codex, Claude Code, Cursor) and see launch videos on X and GitHub.
Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps, 30 s, 120 BPM synth bed (launch preset), no voice-over.

Rules: real footage only (the code page shows the real launch-teaser timeline and the real output of rendering it; scene 3 records the
feature-demo sample app driven by real steps); generated stills only as supporting images; one idea per scene; hard cuts except two 0.25 s
transitions; reading holds of at least 1.5 s for every caption.
